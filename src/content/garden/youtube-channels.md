# Overview

This is a list of youtube channels that I personally love and that have obscure
or niche appeal.

# List

## 1. Jonas Laursen

[Jonas Laursen](https://www.youtube.com/@jonasblaursen) is a danish guy who
makes videos on international politics. He mostly does videos on more obscure
elections and the political oddities of smaller countries that get less
coverage. I really like his series of videos where he methodically explains
history and ideological tendencies of each political party of a country.

## 2. Mathematical Adventures

[Mathematical Adventures](https://www.youtube.com/@mathematicaladventures) is a
great channel for learning advanced math. He got a PhD in particle physics but
makes videos on self-learning some of the more advanced mathematics beyond just
the math needed for physics. I love his videos where he reviews a book from the
perspective of a self-taught layman trying to learn a new field of math.
