When setting up a new server (or desktop) I like to give it a name of some
historic train. This is my list of train names to use, plus some interesting
info about trains lmao. My criteria is that the name has to be one word
(sometimes I will just use one word of a multi-word name) and has to be sort and
cool sounding.

These are just my favorite. See [the wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_named_passenger_trains) for the big
list.

* zephyr: An [amtrak train](https://www.amtrak.com/california-zephyr-train) in
  going from San Francisco to Chicago. Zephyr is also a pretty common train name
  in the US.
* [Comet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_(train))
* [Zenith](/static/files/AAR%20Names%20and%20Nicknames%20of%20Freight%20Trains%202-1952.pdf)
([source](https://www.multimodalways.org/docs/railroads/RRorgs/AAR/AAR%20Names%20and%20Nicknames%20of%20Freight%20Trains%202-1952.pdf))
* [The Ghan](https://www.journeybeyondrail.com.au/the-ghan/) crosses Australia.
* [cascadia](https://www.cascadiarail.org/) is the plan for high speed rail in
  Seattle. This is in the US so it probably won't happen but I can dream (and its
  a good name).
* [sibirjak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibirjak) Ran from Berlin to Kazakhstan until 2013.
* marquette: The Pere Marquette is a train from Grand Rapids in operation from
  1947 to 1971.
* avrora: Romanized version of Авро́ра which means aurora in Russian. A
  [double-decker train in moscow](https://rollingstockworld.com/passenger-cars/rzd-puts-in-operation-revived-aurora-passenger-train/) in operation from 1963 to 2010.
* Italmas: Romanized version of Италмас is an RŽD train who's name means [globeflower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trollius_europaeus).
* Strizh: A high-speed locomotive. Strizh is the anglicised version of Стриж
  which is the russian name for the apodidae bird (also called swifts), a really
  fast bird.
* [Seminole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seminole_(train))
* Kuaisu: Romanized Chinese for "fast" (快速) is used in the naming convention for some high speed rail.
* Tekuai: Romanized Chinese for "express" (特快) is used in the naming convention for some high speed rail.
